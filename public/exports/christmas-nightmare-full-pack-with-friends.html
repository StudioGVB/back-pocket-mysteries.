Christmas Nightmare

They all grew up together on the same street: two sisters and two neighbour brothers whose lives have been intertwined since childhood. Tonight, beneath the twinkling lights of their annual Christmas dinner party, years of tangled history, secret affairs, and broken marriages finally snap. When a sudden 8:19 PM power surge plunges the living room into darkness and Luke is discovered dead in the Christmas tree corner, amid a spilled cup of spiced eggnog, a scorched wall outlet, and a tangled coil of vintage tree lights, the holiday reunion turns into a Christmas Nightmare. Everyone in the room is family, a childhood neighbour, a spouse, or a secret lover, and every single one of them is hiding a betrayal.
